As 2024 unfolds, I’m stepping further into the electrifying world of stage photography. This year marks a deeper commitment to capturing the raw energy, emotion, and artistry of live performances. What makes this journey even more special is photographing bands whose music I’ve cherished since middle school—a true full-circle moment. Along the way, I’ve discovered that what I love most about stage photography is capturing the crowd—their energy, connection, and raw emotion bring every shot to life. From intimate gigs to grand productions, every frame tells a story, and I’m here to preserve these fleeting moments with passion and precision. Here's to a year of growth, exploration, and unforgettable experiences behind the lens.
