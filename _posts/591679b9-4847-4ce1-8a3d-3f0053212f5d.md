As 2024 unfolds, I’m stepping further into the electrifying world of stage photography. This year marks a deeper commitment to capturing the raw energy, emotion, and artistry of live performances. What makes this journey even more special is photographing bands whose music I’ve cherished since middle school—a true full-circle moment.

This year, I’ve found myself photographing more international musicians than local ones, with a tendency toward wide-angle shots that capture the concert atmosphere. Perhaps this stems from a strong fascination with the “atmosphere of concerts” or the influence of street photography, which still shapes much of my approach. However, a lack of research into the performers has caused me to miss some truly epic moments.

Looking ahead to 2025, it’s time to explore a more intimate style of documentation—getting closer to the musicians and breaking free from the constraints of atmosphere- and street-inspired photography. This shift aims to create more diverse and impactful work that truly captures the essence of live performances.

From intimate gigs to grand productions, every frame tells a story, and I’m here to preserve these fleeting moments with passion and precision. Here's to a year of growth, exploration, and unforgettable experiences behind the lens.
